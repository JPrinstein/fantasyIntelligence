Quarterbacks in Fantasy Football

Quarterbacks (QBs) generally score fantasy points through passing yards, passing touchdowns, rushing yards, and rushing touchdowns. Interceptions and fumbles can reduce a quarterback's fantasy score depending on league settings.

Passing volume is an important factor when evaluating quarterbacks. A quarterback who attempts many passes has more opportunities to accumulate passing yards and passing touchdowns.

Rushing ability can significantly increase a quarterback's fantasy value. Rushing yards generally provide fantasy points, and rushing touchdowns are often worth more than rushing yards alone.

A quarterback's fantasy performance can be affected by offensive scheme, passing volume, supporting players, offensive line performance, game script, opponent strength, and injuries.

When evaluating a quarterback, useful information includes passing attempts, completions, passing yards, passing touchdowns, interceptions, rushing attempts, rushing yards, rushing touchdowns, and recent performance.

Quarterback rankings should also consider the league's scoring settings. For example, a quarterback may have different relative value in a league that heavily rewards passing touchdowns compared with a league that gives greater value to rushing production.
