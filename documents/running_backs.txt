Running Backs in Fantasy Football

Running backs (RBs) are fantasy football players who primarily gain value through rushing attempts, rushing yards, rushing touchdowns, receptions, and receiving yards.

A running back who receives a large number of rushing attempts is often described as having a high workload or high volume. Volume is an important factor when evaluating running backs because more opportunities can lead to more fantasy points.

Receiving work can be especially valuable for running backs in PPR and half-PPR leagues. A running back who catches several passes per game can produce useful fantasy points even when the player does not have a strong rushing performance.

Goal-line opportunities are also important. Running backs who regularly receive carries near the opponent's goal line have increased opportunities to score rushing touchdowns.

A running back's fantasy value can be affected by the team's offensive system, the player's role, competition from other running backs, injuries, game script, and the strength of the opposing defense.

When evaluating a running back, useful information includes rushing attempts, rushing yards, rushing touchdowns, receptions, receiving yards, targets, red-zone opportunities, and recent workload.
